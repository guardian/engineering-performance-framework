# Senior Engineering Manager

Manages performance of a significant number of individuals (6 to 9). May manage multiple teams.

## People

The expectations of the Engineering Manager level _and:_

### Engineering management team

- Leads departmental initiatives and improves management processes across the department.
- Seeks opportunities to coach and offers support to less experienced managers.

### Line Management

- Improves people management across the department through discussions with other managers and the coaching of less experienced managers.

### Inclusion

- Ensures that inclusion is considered in department wide management practices.

## Delivery

The expectations of the Engineering Manager level _and:_

### Delivery Pace

- Leads delivery across multiple teams or streams of work.
- Delegates work in a way that provides growth opportunities for team members.
- Accountable for the architecture of the systems owned by multiple teams.
- Delegates architecture responsibility appropriately to ensure teams are able to deliver sustainably.

### Health

- Supports other engineering managers in finding the right balance of health and product work and communicates the importance of health across multiple teams.

### Inclusion

- Promotes inclusive management practices to support delivery across the department, for example, by making sure that growth opportunities are evenly distributed.

## Leadership

The expectations of the Engineering Manager level _and:_

### Team

- Stays connected to the detail and is able to participate in technical discussions.

### Stakeholders

- Is aware of the wider business context and is able to communicate this to the team Leads resolution of complex issues through discussions with senior stakeholders, as required.
- Makes an active effort to build relationships, trust and influence with key stakeholders and is able to communicate work that their teams do in a business context.
