# Engineering Manager

Line manages a significant number of individuals (\~6)

## People

The expectations of the Associate level _and:_

### Line management

- Proactively supports line reports’ growth, nurtures good performance and takes active steps to manage underperformance.
- Is able to implement PIPs when appropriate and deliver on their outcomes with little support from their manager.
- Seeks out new opportunities to support the growth and/or career interests of line reports, where appropriate.

### Engineering management team

- Participates actively in departmental initiatives.
- Participates actively in EM group discussions.

### Inclusion

- Creates an inclusive environment in their team, ensures that everyone’s voice is heard and adapts ways of working to enable their team to do their best work.

## Delivery

The expectations of the Associate level _and:_

### Delivery pace

- Collaborates on continuously improving and maintaining the delivery pace of their team and evolves technical and team practices to support this.
- Able to balance the team’s short term goals with long term goals.
- Ensures delivery pace remains sustainable and aligned to broader strategic goals. Suggests solutions to potential delays to projects while being clear on the tradeoffs involved.

### Health

- Ensures the right balance between Health and feature delivery, engaging other team leads in the process.

### Inclusion

- Ensures all team members are provided with learning and growth opportunities, as an investment in future delivery performance.

## Leadership

The expectations of the Associate level _and:_

### Team

- Ensures that the team progress and achievements are communicated to the department as appropriate.
- Empowers all members of their team to make decisions and ensures everyone’s voice is heard.
- Participates in defining the team’s direction with product manager.
- Provides strategic technical stewardship to accelerate team delivery and foster engineering excellence, balancing hands-on contributions with the need to remain unblocked from critical path activities, thereby maximising team impact.

### Stakeholders

- Communicates technical challenges and opportunities in the domain area of their team in a clear way to stakeholders.
- Communicates the importance of health for sustainability to stakeholders.
