# Associate Engineering Manager

Line manages a of small number of individuals (\~3)

## People

### People management

- Guides career development and progression of line reports by setting objectives with them and aligning their objectives with career performance framework.
- Sets new joiners up for success and ensures that offboarding of individuals is smooth (manages effectively the full lifecycle of developers from hiring to joining to offboarding).
- Demonstrates people management skills such as motivating, coaching, mentoring and empowering.
- Manages performance concerns with support from their manager and recognises when a PIP is an appropriate course of action.

### Engineering management team

- Participates actively in departmental initiatives led by managers.
- Contributes to broader engineering team management by participating in performance review and promotion discussions, reading and sharing feedback for individuals outside of their team, where relevant.

### Inclusion

- Discusses inclusivity with their line reports where relevant or appropriate, helping those who exhibit non-inclusive behaviour to improve in this area.

## Delivery

### Delivery pace

- Enables effective decision making within the team Organises the team’s work, sets goals and milestones, removes blockers and keeps the team on track.
- \[ASSOCIATE ONLY\] Regularly contributes technically to the team in a way that’s sensitive to the needs of the team, this can range from code, reviews, architectural leadership or discussion.
- Communicates project estimates, progress and delays to stakeholders proactively.

### Health

- Ensures the team looks after system health e.g. by participating in defining health tasks.

### Inclusion

- Makes sure that work in the team is distributed fairly and everyone has a voice in decision-making.

## Leadership

### Team

- Aligns the team’s work with wider business goals, for example by supporting the team in prioritising their work and making sure that work is scoped appropriately.
- Builds relationships with other relevant disciplines within the team and makes sure decision making is aligned.
- Leads by example by using and advocating for the use of best development practices.
- Participates in defining key metrics for systems owned by team and is able to use these metrics appropriately to guide the team’s work

### Inclusion

- Considers the impact of their own behaviour on their team’s culture.
- Demonstrates an understanding of inclusive management practices, the importance of inclusive teams, and the impact of decision-making on inclusion within their team.
